Common questions

Frequently asked questions.

Do you work with international organizations that have no presence in India?

Yes — this is one of our core areas of work. We can act as your on-ground team for hiring, office setup, and day-to-day operations, so you don't need to establish a full local entity to start working in India.

Can you support a project in a specific state or region?

Yes. We're based in Delhi NCR and can coordinate staff and activities across states depending on your programme's needs — get in touch with the specific location and we'll confirm feasibility.

Do you only work with health-focused programmes?

Health is where we're rooted, but our ground support, HR, admin, and reporting services apply to development, research, and NGO/CSR programmes more broadly — not just clinical or healthcare work.

How quickly can you start on a new engagement?

It depends on scope — a discovery call is the fastest way to get a clear answer for your specific timeline. Reach out by call or WhatsApp and we'll respond quickly.

Can you handle just one part of this — say, only HR, or only reporting?

Yes, our services aren't all-or-nothing. You can bring us in for a single piece of work, like report preparation or a dashboard build, without committing to full on-ground operations support.
