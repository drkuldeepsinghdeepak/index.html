For doctors & researchers

Support beyond the ground.

Alongside patient care, we support clinicians and medical students with the academic work that comes with practicing medicine.

Case discussion

Case report discussion

Structured review and discussion of clinical case reports — useful for teaching rounds, documentation, or preparing a case for publication.

How we do this
  • We review the case details and relevant literature together with you beforehand.
  • We help structure the discussion around clinical reasoning, differentials, and key learning points.
  • Useful for teaching rounds, or as groundwork for a future publication.
Research writing

Proposal writing support

Help structuring and refining research proposals, from study design through to a submission-ready document.

How we do this
  • We start by clarifying your research question and study design with you.
  • We structure the proposal to match the funder or institution's specific format and requirements.
  • We review drafts and refine the language before submission.
Publication

Manuscript publication support

Guidance through drafting, formatting, and submitting manuscripts to medical journals.

How we do this
  • We help structure your manuscript to match your target journal's guidelines.
  • We support drafting, reference formatting, and preparing figures and tables.
  • We assist with the submission process and responding to reviewer comments.
